Meet Elena

Nutrition care that meets you where you are.

I'm Elena Pevar, a registered dietitian and the founder of Remedy Nutrition. I started this practice with a simple belief: food should help you feel better — not become one more source of stress in your life.

Many of the people I work with arrive exhausted by conflicting advice and diets that didn't last. My approach is different. Together, we'll look at the whole picture — your health history, your schedule, your culture, and the foods you genuinely enjoy — and build a plan grounded in science that fits your real life.

  • Evidence-based

    Recommendations grounded in current research, not trends.

  • Whole-person

    Your history, lifestyle, and preferences all shape the plan.

  • Sustainable

    Small, realistic changes that last long after our sessions.

Services

Support designed around your life

Every plan is built for you — your goals, your health history, and the way you actually eat. All sessions are held through secure telehealth, so you can meet from the comfort of home.

60–75 minutes

Initial Consultation

A comprehensive first visit where we look at the full picture — your health history, labs, current eating habits, and lifestyle — and set clear, realistic priorities together.

  • In-depth nutrition assessment
  • Review of labs, medications & supplements
  • Personalized plan and next steps

30–45 minutes

Follow-Up Sessions

Regular check-ins to build on your progress, work through challenges, and adjust your plan as your needs and goals evolve.

  • Progress review and accountability
  • Plan adjustments as you go
  • Practical meal and snack ideas

3-month programs

Care Packages

A bundled program for deeper, lasting change — consistent sessions plus support between visits, so you're never figuring it out alone.

  • Initial consultation plus follow-ups
  • Between-session messaging support
  • Custom resources, recipes & guides

Specialties

Areas of focus

Whether you're managing a diagnosis or simply want more energy day to day, we'll build a plan that fits.

  • Gut Health & Digestion

    Relief for IBS, bloating, and reflux, including guided approaches like low-FODMAP.

  • Diabetes & Blood Sugar

    Practical strategies for prediabetes, type 1 and type 2, and gestational diabetes.

  • Heart Health

    Eating patterns that support healthy cholesterol and blood pressure — without sacrificing flavor.

  • PCOS & Hormone Health

    Support for PCOS, insulin resistance, and the transitions of perimenopause and menopause.

  • Relationship With Food

    An intuitive eating approach for anyone ready to step off the dieting cycle for good.

  • Prenatal & Postpartum

    Nourishing yourself through fertility, pregnancy, breastfeeding, and the months after.

  • Food Allergies & Sensitivities

    Identifying triggers safely and building a varied, satisfying diet around them.

  • Whole-Body Wellness

    Better energy, healthy aging, and prevention-focused nutrition for everyday life.
